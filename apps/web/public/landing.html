Questions people ask.

What is ox.family?

A launchpad on Robinhood Chain that sides with the people who buy and hold. Anyone can make a coin and anyone can trade it. Every trade pays a 1% fee, and 80% of that fee goes back to people: the creator, the holders of the house coin, and the best traders of the hour. People who play dirty pay extra, and that goes to the holders.

What is the Bag?

The pot every fee goes into. It is a contract with no owner and no way to take money out. It can only pay out, and the split is fixed: of every fee it gets, 30 parts go to people who locked the house coin, 10 to the best traders of the hour, 10 to buy and burn the house coin, and 20 to us.

How much does a trade cost?

1% of the trade. A third of that goes to the person who made the coin, the rest goes into the Bag. If the coin was launched straight into a pool, the creator may add his own tax on top, between 1 and 10%, and that part is all his.

What is Payday?

Every hour, part of the fees from that hour is paid to the traders who were most active in it. A slice also goes to the holders of the ten newest coins.

What is the Vault?

You lock the house coin in it. From then on you get a share of every fee on the whole chain, every block, paid in ETH, USDG and stocks. Lock for a year and you get 2.5 times more than someone who did not lock.

What is the burn?

Every hour, part of the fees buys the house coin on the market and destroys it. Fewer coins exist after every hour.

What happens when a coin graduates?

A coin starts on a curve. When enough people have bought it, it graduates: 90% of the money goes into a real pool that stays locked forever. The other 10% is the graduation fee: half to us, a quarter to the Vault, a quarter to the coin holders as a bonus, that same second.

How do holders get paid?

Every coin has its own small pot. Penalties paid by snipers, flippers and whales go in there, 80% of them. Every five minutes the money is pushed to holders. You do not click anything, and we pay the gas.

Can the creator take the money and run?

No. If the creator sells his own coin, all the fees he has not collected yet go to the holders, in the same transaction.

What extra rules can a creator turn on?

A tax on people who sell within minutes of buying. A tax on sells big enough to move the price. King of the hill: 60 seconds with no buy and the last buyer wins a pot. And an auction for the very first buy, with half of the money going to holders.

What does it cost to launch a coin?

0.002 ETH. If you want your coin shown at the top of the board, you can buy an hourly spot.

What money do I get paid in?

Whatever the coin is priced in. There are 235 pairs: ETH, USDG, 192 real stocks like NVDA, TSLA or SPY, and 40 coins from the chain. A coin priced in a stock pays you in that stock.

Is there a house coin?

Yes, it is coming. The Vault and the burn are built around it. It will be launched through the app itself. Until then, their share of the fees waits inside the Bag.
